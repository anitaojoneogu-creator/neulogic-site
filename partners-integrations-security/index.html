Market Infrastructure

Connected to the exchange, certified not co-branded.

Symplus connects directly to NGX’s trading infrastructure. This reflects certified connectivity, not a business partnership with NGX.

Exchange

Nigerian Exchange Group (NGX)

Trade-X provides real-time, FIX-protocol order management and market-data access across all NGX trading boards. When NGX launched derivatives as a new tradeable asset class, Symplus shipped a dedicated derivatives trading module built to support it.

Integration Capabilities

Symplus connects to the systems you already run.

API-Based Integration

An open API interface for integration with any third-party application.

FIX Protocol Connectivity

Real-time order and market-data messaging with multiple markets and trading destinations.

Cross-Module Integration

Securities trading, trust management, and accounting modules are directly integrated with each other, so front-office, back-office, and financial reporting run off the same data.

Compliance & Security

What Symplus does, said plainly.

Rules-Based Compliance Engines

The Asset Management and Derivatives modules include rules-based compliance engines that keep portfolios and trades within defined risk and regulatory parameters. This is a product feature for your own regulatory compliance.

IFRS-Compliant Reporting

The Accounting module produces IFRS-compliant financial statements: Trial Balance, Statement of Financial Position, and Income Statement.

Security-Minded Development

Our mobile development process uses a shift-left approach, building security testing into every stage of the app lifecycle from ideation through release, to protect data integrity in mobile financial transactions.
